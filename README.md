# Richardson Technologies Revenue Dashboard

An interactive sales-performance dashboard for monitoring revenue and order activity across Indian cities, states, and regions.

## Dashboard overview

The dashboard brings together geographical, regional, and order-level views to help users identify where revenue is generated and how order volume relates to sales value.

### Key metrics

- **Orders:** total order count, displayed as a headline KPI.
- **Revenue:** total revenue, displayed as a headline KPI.
- **Revenue by city:** horizontal bar chart comparing revenue across individual cities.
- **Revenue by state:** horizontal bar chart ranking revenue across states.
- **Revenue on map:** map of India showing the geographic distribution of revenue and orders.
- **Revenue pie chart:** revenue share by region: Central, East, North, South, and West.
- **Orders vs Revenue:** scatter plot showing the relationship between order volume and revenue.

## How to use it

1. Start with the Orders and Revenue KPI cards for a quick performance summary.
2. Use the city and state charts to spot top- and low-performing markets.
3. Inspect the map to understand geographic coverage and concentration.
4. Compare regional contributions in the pie chart.
5. Use the Orders vs Revenue plot to find markets with unusually high or low revenue for their order volume.

## Visual conventions

- Darker blue marks indicate relatively higher values.
- Bar lengths represent revenue amounts.
- Map markers communicate location and relative performance.
- The scatter plot positions each market by order count on the horizontal axis and revenue on the vertical axis.

## Intended users

Sales leaders, operations teams, and business analysts can use this dashboard to prioritize markets, review regional performance, and support revenue planning.

## Data notes

Use consistent currency formatting and keep city, state, region, order, and revenue data current. Investigate any unmapped or unknown locations so geographic reporting remains accurate.
